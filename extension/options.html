Rename shelves and edit links

On your site, tap The Library of Links plaque three times and enter your passcode. You can rename and reorder shelves, edit any link, and publish it all at once.

Shortcuts

Alt + L opens Linkmery on any page (Option + L on a Mac). Enter adds the page; Alt + F jumps to Find. In the address bar, type lm and a space to search your library. Change the shortcut at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

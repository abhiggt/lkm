What’s new

Every version of the library, newest first.

V7.3

Cheat codes

3 November 2026
  • Cheat codes: make one in the admin panel, type it exactly into the search bar, and it opens a shelf, a book or any page.
  • Books have depth now, with the pages and the back board showing at the edge.
  • A sheen passes over a shelf’s title when you point at it.
  • See all is a brown leather spine, lettered like the logo.
  • Spines is now Stack, and its books stand taller.
  • Share this shelf moved up, level with the search bar.
  • The footer’s warm light is now a faint line along its top edge.
  • Quieter taps.
V7.2

A little light

3 November 2026
  • The globe is gone. An old filament bulb takes its place: switch it on at night and the room warms up. In daylight, brightness is within.
  • The light and the plant stay in the main hall. Inside a shelf, the cabinet top is clear.
  • The plaque keeps its secrets. Two taps and it says there’s nothing to see here; three and it reminds you.
  • The bookmark on the card is gone.
  • A warm light passes along the footer now and then.
  • The site is now called Linkmery: links’ memory.
  • Rename a shelf from the extension: the pencil beside the shelf, or Shelf names in its settings.
  • The admin panel opens reliably.
V7

Quieter, more personal

3 November 2026
  • Inside a shelf, the plaque becomes a small gold brooch. Tap it to go back to the main hall.
  • Wander replaces Surprise me: a random book from a random shelf.
  • Type all in search to see every shelf at once.
  • On a book’s card, the type opens a hidden shelf of its kind, and the site has a copy button.
  • Go to the site is now a stitched strap in the book’s own leather.
  • A satin ribbon marks each card. Turn it over for a leaf pressed from the plant on top.
  • The globe only spins. The plant counts today’s waterings and gets a few drops at a time.
  • See all is a cloth spine. Covers, label holders and buttons feel more like real things.
  • Fewer sounds, and every page works on a phone.
  • The extension is calmer and grey with a touch of gold. It says Add rather than Shelve, and you can try it on its preview page.
  • New address: abhiggt.github.io/lkm.
V6

A realistic library

  • A desk globe that turns on its tilted axis, and a real money plant, both drawn as images.
  • Bigger, clearer type on the cards. Fraunces is back for the shelf names.
  • Shelf titles on brass label holders, large enough to tap.
  • “Why I made this” fell out of a book and into the footer, as a bookmark.
  • Three taps on the plaque open the admin panel.
V5

A library someone keeps

  • Seven books on each shelf at home, then See all.
  • A brass plaque on the top rail names the library.
  • In Spines view you can pick a book up and move it along the shelf.
  • Catalogue numbers, stamps and clutter are gone, and nothing sits behind an open card.
V4

Books open their card

  • Tapping a book never leaves the page. It turns over into a card with two buttons.
  • Quieter sounds made from real textures: paper, wood, a latch, a drop of water.
  • Real shadows and depth on the shelves.
V3

Covers in their own colours

  • Each cover fades into the colour of its site’s favicon.
  • A book flip, a back button, dark mode fixes, and a first deploy.
V2

Glass doors

  • Books wear their favicons, and a plant lives on top of the bookcase.

The first shelves

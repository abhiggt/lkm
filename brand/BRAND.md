# Linkmery brand

**Name.** Linkmery blends almari (अलमारी, cupboard), library, and "link meri" (my links). Always lowercase in the wordmark, "Linkmery" in sentences.

**Idea.** A glass-front almari: visitors look in, search and take a copy; only the owner rearranges the shelves. The read-only showcase and the metaphor are the same thing.

**Mark.** Three books on a shelf, two standing and one leaning, drawn with one thin rounded stroke on a 32 px grid. The floor plan on the site uses the same stroke weight.
- Do: use it in one colour, ink on white by day, light on black by night.
- Do: keep clear space of at least one book width around it.
- Don't: fill the books, add colour per book, outline the wordmark, or redraw the lean.

**Colour.** White and black pages, light and dark grey shelves, one accent: almari brass (#8a6417 by day, #d2a95a by night), from the key and the lock. Tokens live in `assets/css/tokens.css`.

**Type.** Instrument Sans for everything, IBM Plex Mono only for domains and dates. Both open-licensed and self-hosted.

**Time.** The site follows India Standard Time for every visitor: light 06:00 to 18:00, dark otherwise.

**Voice.** Short, warm, a little quirky, never cute. Plain verbs, sentence case.
- "Search the almari"
- "Pull one out"
- "Nothing on these shelves for that. Did you mean colour?"
- "This page has moved out. The archived copy still opens."
- "It's 9:40 pm in India, so the almari is dark."

**Writing descriptions.** One or two sentences: what it is, and when you'd reach for it, in the words you'd search with later.

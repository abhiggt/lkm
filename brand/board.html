Linkmery brand board

Linkmery blends almari (अलमारी, cupboard), library and "link meri" (my links). The idea: a glass-front almari. Visitors look in and take a copy of anything; only the owner rearranges the shelves. Reply with picks by number, for example "logo 1, type A, accent 1, voice ok".

Logo concepts

Each mark is drawn on a 32 px grid with one monoline stroke, the same line the floor plan uses. Shown at five sizes on the day and night themes, with a lockup and a browser tab.
